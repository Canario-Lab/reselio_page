Reselio Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026 · Version: 2.0

1. Controller and contact

Canario-Lab / Reselio App controls personal data that we process. Contact: reselio@reselio.app.

2. Service scope

Reselio runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac and records resale purchases, inventory, listings, sales, expenses, photos, and results. We do not create Reselio user accounts.

3. User data and iCloud

Business data is stored locally and, when available, synchronized through the private CloudKit database of the user's iCloud account. This includes items, units, sales, expenses, photos, categories, platforms, operation history needed for safe merging, language, currency, and free-sale usage. Canario-Lab has no account dashboard or access to browse this private database.

App lock, view layout, active filters, reminder settings, and the date of the last local backup remain device-local. The iCloud account used for data may differ from the App Store account used for purchases.

On first launch the App may wait for an iCloud import. The user may explicitly continue offline; later changes will merge. Technical conflict and undone-operation records may remain in the private database to preserve consistency across devices.

4. Backups and files

A manual backup is created on request and saved to a location selected by the user. Backup v3 contains App data, a generation identifier, protocol version, and integrity checksum. Restoring replaces local state and may then synchronize it to other devices. iCloud does not replace an independent backup because it also synchronizes deletions.

5. System access

Reselio may use the camera and photo library, file picker, system sharing, iCloud/CloudKit, and Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode. Access is used only for the feature selected by the user. Biometric data is never provided to Reselio.

6. Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple handles payments through the App Store and StoreKit. RevenueCat presents offers, verifies Premium, reconciles receipts across devices, and restores purchases. Product and transaction identifiers, subscription state, entitlement dates, and a technical RevenueCat identifier may be processed. We do not receive card details.

7. Analytics

Release builds may use Firebase Analytics for anonymous feature events such as adding an item, recording a sale, listing an item, adding an expense, or opening Summary. We do not send names, notes, photos, links, monetary amounts, prices, costs, backup contents, or exports. Analytics is not used for advertising or selling data and is disabled in UI-test mode.

8. Link import and website

When the user imports a listing, the App contacts the supplied public URL to retrieve available metadata and images. The destination operator may receive standard connection data. Hosting for reselio.app may process IP address, browser type, and request time in security logs.

9. Recipients

Recipients may include Apple (iCloud, App Store, and system features), RevenueCat (purchases), Google Firebase (anonymous analytics), email providers when support is contacted, and public authorities where legally required.

10. Retention

App data remains on devices and in private iCloud until deleted by the user or under Apple settings. The free-sale ledger is grow-only so undoing a sale does not renew the allowance. Support correspondence is retained for handling the request and any necessary claims period.

11. Rights

Where we process personal data, such as support email, users have applicable GDPR rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and complaint to a supervisory authority. Users control private iCloud data through the App and Apple settings.

12. Security and children

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Users must also protect their device, Apple account, and backups. The App is not directed to children under 13.

13. Changes

The current policy is available in the App and may be published at reselio.app. Material changes will accompany an App or document update.
